Loads actual MNIST data from Google's public sample dataset. Training happens locally in your browser; an internet connection is required to load the dataset and TensorFlow.js.

For a responsive demo, training uses a subset of MNIST; evaluation uses 500 disjoint held-out examples. This does not represent full 10,000-image MNIST test-set accuracy.
