How the network makes a prediction

Each of the 512 hidden neurons forms a weighted sum of both inputs and a bias, then applies ReLU. The output neuron combines their activations and converts the result to a probability.

Hidden unit j: hⱼ = max(0, wⱼ₁x₁ + wⱼ₂x₂ + bⱼ)

Output: ŷ = sigmoid(b + Σⱼ vⱼhⱼ)
